# Kestrel Freightworks — Leave Policy

Policy owner: People Operations (Farida Oyelaran, Head of People)
Version: 4.2, effective 1 April 2026. Replaces version 4.1.

## 1. Annual leave

- Every full-time employee gets **23 days** of annual leave per calendar year.
- Employees with more than 5 years of service get **2 extra days** ("loyalty days").
- Up to **7 unused days** can be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward days expire on **30 June** of the next year.
- Leave requests longer than 4 consecutive working days must be raised at least **15 days** in advance in the Perch portal.

## 2. Sick leave

- **11 days** of paid sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than **3 consecutive days**.
- Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## 3. Special leave

| Leave type | Days | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Parental leave (primary carer) | 26 weeks | Fully paid |
| Parental leave (secondary carer) | 6 weeks | Fully paid, can be split into two blocks |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days | Immediate family |
| Marriage leave | 4 days | Once during employment |
| Volunteering day ("Kestrel Gives Back") | 2 days | Must be with a registered non-profit |
| Exam leave | 3 days per exam | Only for company-sponsored certifications |

## 4. Kestrel Quiet Week

The whole company shuts down for **Quiet Week**, the last week of December. Quiet Week does **not** count against annual leave. The on-call rota still runs during Quiet Week (see the On-call Runbook).

## 5. Changes from version 4.1

- Annual leave increased from 21 to 23 days.
- Carry-forward limit reduced from 10 days to 7 days.
- Volunteering leave increased from 1 day to 2 days.
