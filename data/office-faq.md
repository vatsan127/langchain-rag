# Kestrel Freightworks — Office FAQ

## Where are the offices?

- **Head office:** "The Nest", 4th floor, Sarjapur Road, Bengaluru.
- **Pune office:** Baner, mainly the Puffin hardware team.
- **Warehouse lab:** Hoskote, where Gannet printers and Puffin scanners are tested.

## What are the office hours?

The Nest is open 07:30 to 21:00 on weekdays. It is **closed on Saturdays and Sundays**. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST; meetings should not be booked outside these hours.

## How many days must I come to the office?

Hybrid employees come in **3 days a week**. **Tuesday is "Flock Day"**: everyone in the hybrid programme comes in on Tuesday. The other two days are your choice.

## How do I get a visitor pass?

Raise a visitor request in Perch at least **24 hours** before the visit. Visitors must carry a government photo ID. The reception desk is on the 4th floor.

## Is there food in the office?

Lunch is free on Flock Day (Tuesday). On other days the cafeteria is subsidised; a meal costs ₹90.

## How do I get IT help?

- Raise a ticket in Perch under "IT > Service Desk".
- Urgent laptop issues: call extension **4400**.
- Warehouse IT desk (Hoskote, Gannet and Puffin devices): extension **4402**.
