# Kestrel Freightworks — Travel and Expense Policy

Policy owner: Finance (Rohan Castellino, Finance Controller)
Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Approvals

| Amount per claim | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹15,000 | Line manager |
| ₹15,001 to ₹1,00,000 | Line manager and department head |
| Above ₹1,00,000 | Finance Controller |

Claims must be submitted in Perch within **45 days** of the expense. Late claims are rejected unless the Finance Controller approves an exception.

## 2. Travel

- Domestic flights: economy class only. Book through the travel desk (travel@kestrelfreight.example).
- International flights longer than **7 hours**: premium economy is allowed.
- Trains: AC 2-tier for journeys over 6 hours.
- Cabs: use the corporate Ola/Uber account. Personal cab claims need a receipt.

## 3. Daily allowance

| City tier | Hotel limit per night | Meals per day |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru) | ₹8,500 | ₹2,200 |
| Tier 2 (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) | ₹6,000 | ₹1,700 |
| Other cities | ₹4,200 | ₹1,300 |

Warehouse site visits (any city) get an extra **₹600 per day** for safety gear and local travel.

## 4. Home office

- One-time home office allowance: **₹22,000**, for employees in the hybrid or remote programme.
- Internet reimbursement: **₹1,500 per month**.

## 5. Not reimbursed

- Alcohol, except at client dinners approved in advance by a department head.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Upgrades paid with personal loyalty points are allowed, but the points themselves are not reimbursed.
