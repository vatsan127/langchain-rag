# Kestrel Freightworks — Product Catalogue 2026

Kestrel Freightworks sells software and hardware for mid-size warehouses and freight forwarders. Products are named after birds.

## Heron — Shipment Orchestration Platform

- Cloud platform that plans, routes and tracks shipments.
- Core services: `heron-planner`, `heron-sorter`, `heron-tracker`.
- Pricing: **₹18 per shipment**, minimum **₹2,40,000 per month**.
- Current version: **Heron 7.3** (released 9 June 2026).
- Uptime commitment: **99.92%** per calendar month. Below that, the customer gets a 10% credit on that month's bill.

## Gannet — Label Printer

- Thermal label printer for parcels, prints 210 labels per minute.
- Price: **₹74,500** per unit, including a 2-year warranty.
- Extended warranty: ₹9,800 per extra year, maximum 3 extra years.
- Connects to Heron over Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

## Puffin — Handheld Scanner

- Rugged barcode scanner running Puffin OS 5.
- Price: **₹38,200** per unit.
- Battery lasts **14 hours**; a spare battery costs ₹4,100.
- Drop-tested from 2.4 metres onto concrete.

## Osprey — Route Analytics (beta)

- Analytics add-on for Heron that predicts late deliveries.
- In closed beta since May 2026 with three customers: Balasore Cold Chain, Velankanni Movers and Dhruva Agro Exports.
- General availability is planned for **February 2027**.
- Beta customers pay nothing until general availability.

## Discontinued

- **Kite** (old desktop tracking app) was discontinued on 31 January 2026. Kite customers are being moved to Heron at no extra cost until 31 December 2026.
